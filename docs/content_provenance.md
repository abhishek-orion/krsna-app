# Content Provenance and Attribution

This document tracks the sources, licenses, and usage rights for all content used in the KRSNA app.

## Bhagavad Gita - English Translation

### Primary Source

- **Title**: The Song Celestial, or Bhagavad-Gita
- **Translator**: Sir Edwin Arnold (1832-1904)
- **Original Publication**: 1885
- **Source URL**: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/2388
- **Provider**: Project Gutenberg
- **License**: Public Domain (US)
- **Jurisdiction**: 
  - Public Domain in the United States (published before 1929)
  - Author deceased more than 70 years (life+70 rule)
- **Format**: Plain text / HTML
- **Language**: English (poetic translation from Sanskrit)

### License Terms

### Attribution

The app displays:
- Translator name: "Sir Edwin Arnold"
- Translation title: "The Song Celestial"

### Usage Notes

- The translation is used verbatim from the Project Gutenberg edition
- No modifications have been made to the actual translation text
- Chapter and verse divisions follow standard Gita structure
- Sanskrit transliterations and original shlokas sourced from public domain references

## Verification Process

For each content source, we verify:

1. **Publication Date**: Before 1929 for US public domain
2. **Author Death Date**: More than 70 years ago (life+70 rule)
3. **License Terms**: Explicit public domain dedication or permissive license
4. **Jurisdiction**: Status in US, India, and other relevant jurisdictions
5. **Edition Rights**: Ensure translation/edition rights, not just original text

## In-App Attribution
